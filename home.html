Meet Our Crew

Chief Executive Officer

Mr. Ravindu Senanayake

As Chief Executive Officer, Ravindu sets the overall direction and vision for Altura AI Technologies. He leads strategic decision-making, oversees fundraising efforts, and builds key partnerships that drive the company's growth. Working closely with every department, he ensures the company stays aligned with its mission of making enterprise-grade automation accessible to businesses of every size.

Chief Technology Officer

Mrs. Dilani Wickramasinghe

Dilani leads all engineering, product development, and technical architecture at Altura AI. With a strong background in machine learning and systems design, she ensures the platform remains scalable, secure, and reliable as the company expands into new markets. She works hand in hand with the engineering team to translate company vision into working technology.

Chief Financial Officer

Mr. Hasitha Fernando

Hasitha oversees financial planning, budgeting, and investor relations for Altura AI. Drawing on his prior experience in venture capital, he ensures the company's growth is financially sustainable while maintaining strong relationships with current and prospective investors. He plays a central role in shaping the company's fundraising strategy.

Chief Marketing Officer

Mrs. Amindu Perera

Amindu drives brand strategy, customer acquisition, and market positioning for Altura AI. Having previously scaled marketing programs at two early-stage startups, he brings a results-driven approach to growing the company's presence and reputation across South Asia and the Middle East.

Chief Operating Officer

Mr. Tharindu Rajapaksha

Tharindu manages day-to-day operations across Altura AI, ensuring smooth execution between engineering, sales, and customer support teams. He has built scalable operational processes at two prior startups and focuses on turning company strategy into efficient, repeatable systems as Altura AI scales.

Chief Product Officer

Mrs. Nadeesha Gunawardena

Nadeesha owns the product roadmap and user experience direction for Altura AI's platform. With a background in product design and customer research, she ensures every feature shipped solves a real problem for the company's clients, balancing innovation with usability.

VP of Sales & Business Development

Mr. Kasun Abeysekara

Kasun leads the sales team and drives new business partnerships for Altura AI. He previously closed enterprise deals at a leading SaaS company and brings a consultative approach to helping clients understand how automation can transform their operations.

Head of People & Culture

Mrs. Senuri Jayawardena

Senuri oversees hiring, onboarding, and company culture as Altura AI's team grows. She is focused on building a remote-first culture that attracts top talent across the region while keeping the team aligned with the company's mission and values.
